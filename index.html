What we do

Bent TV is produced by Bent TV Inc and made by community for community. The show carries interview-format segments, a two-host film discussion, trans community advocacy, and queer health and wellbeing conversations.

New episodes weekly across community television in Melbourne, Geelong and Adelaide, plus streaming on CTV+ and YouTube.
