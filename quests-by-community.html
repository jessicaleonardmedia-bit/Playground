Segment - Health and wellbeing

Quests by Community
(Our Health Matters)

An interview-format segment focusing on LGBTQIA+ health and wellbeing. Episodes frequently cover topics that need content warnings, including mental health, suicide, HIV/AIDS, family and domestic violence, and sexual violence.

Format

Interview between host and guest, single guest per episode.

If an episode raises something for you

You can reach QLife for free LGBTI peer support, or Switchboard Victoria for peer-driven support services. More crisis and support links on our community resources page.

Posts from this segment use the hashtags #QuestsByCommunity and #OurHealthMatters.
