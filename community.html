Community resources

Organisations Bent TV regularly links to. If something an episode raised needs talking through, or you want to find your way into the community, these are good places to start.

  • QLife

    QLife provides anonymous and free LGBTI peer support and referral for people in Australia wanting to talk about sexuality, identity, gender, bodies, feelings, or relationships.

  • Switchboard Victoria

    Switchboard Victoria provides peer-driven support services for the lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender and gender diverse, intersex, queer and asexual (LGBTIQA+) people, their families, allies, and communities.

  • Minus18

    Changing the Lives Of LGBTIQA+ Youth in Australia.

  • Victorian Pride Centre

    Australia's first purpose-built pride centre. Hosts events and runs a volunteer program.

  • JOY 94.9

    JOY 94.9 is Melbourne's LGBTIQA+ community media. Find out the different ways to listen on their about page or browse their services.

  • GLOBE

    Connecting and Nurturing Victoria's LGBTIQ+ communities, small businesses, and professionals.
